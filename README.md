# Élan — French digital marketing agency concept

Buildless HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Open `dist/index.html`, or serve `dist` with any static server.

Includes responsive navigation, service accordions, project filters, accessible native dialogs, FAQ and a local project brief download. No backend, analytics or tracking cookies. Google Fonts is the only external asset service.

`dist/research.html` documents the ten agency websites reviewed on 17 October 2026 and how their content/structure influenced the design.

Élan is a provisional brand. Portfolio work is fictional and labelled as concepts. Replace agency identity, legal information and portfolio with verified business information before commercial launch. The brief form downloads a text file and does not send enquiries. Connect a real delivery service when the business contact details are supplied.
